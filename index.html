Common questions

Where are my saved leagues?

Sign in with the same iCloud account. Development and production data are separate. An independently imported Sleeper league is a separate RANKD workspace from a shared workspace invitation.

Is RANKD free?

The current iOS version has no ads, subscriptions, or purchases. All league tools are free. Commissioner permissions still apply to official league settings and publishing. If you previously subscribed, manage any existing billing in Apple subscription settings; updating or deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do writeups work?

Stat-based reports run on your device. For optional AI writing, open Weekly Rankings → Write with My AI, select your voice, review and copy the prompt, then paste it into your preferred AI app. Bring the response back to RANKD, review it, and save it. Choose League Only for plain text, or League & Teams for structured JSON with each team matched correctly. RANKD makes no paid AI requests; your provider may have its own charges or limits. Saved reports are readable by members of the same workspace and can be shared as graphics.

How do I delete my account?

Open More → Delete account after signing in, or see deletion help.

Common questions

Where are my saved leagues?

Sign in with the same iCloud account. Development and production data are separate. An independently imported Sleeper league is a separate RANKD workspace from a shared workspace invitation.

How do I restore Pro?

Sign into the RANKD account associated with your purchase, open the RANKD Pro tab, and choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription. Contact support if access is still missing.

How do I cancel Pro?

Use Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app or your RANKD account does not cancel billing.

How do AI writeups work?

Pro members can explicitly generate reports within their allowance. Members of the same shared workspace can read saved reports for free. Viewing or sharing saved writing does not generate a new AI request. Stat-based writing remains available.

How do I delete my account?

Open More → Delete account after signing in, or see deletion help.

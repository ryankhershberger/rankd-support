Delete Your RANKD Account

Sign in to RANKD, open More → Delete account, and confirm. Deletion removes your owned workspaces and account-associated data as described in our Privacy Policy.

Cancel billing separately

Account deletion does not cancel RANKD Pro. Manage or cancel it in Apple subscription settings.

Cannot access the app?

Email ryankhershberger@gmail.com with the subject “RANKD account deletion.” Describe the problem and how we can identify your RANKD account. We will help verify ownership without asking for your Apple password. Do not post account details in public GitHub issues.

Shared and exported content

Public links from your deleted owned workspaces stop working. Other users' independently imported workspaces, previously exported images, and required provider/security records may remain. Deletion does not delete your Apple or Sleeper account.

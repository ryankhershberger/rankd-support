Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026 · Applies to RANKD for iOS and its related web services

RANKD is developed and operated by Ryan Hershberger ("RANKD," "we," or "us"). This policy explains the information used to provide fantasy football rankings, saved league workspaces, sharing, and optional writing imported from your own AI app. Privacy questions: hershapplications@gmail.com.

The essentials

RANKD for iOS is free, with no ads or in-app purchases. Apple handles iCloud sign-in; we do not receive your Apple password. Rankings and stat-based writing run without an AI request. Write with My AI lets you review and copy a prompt into your own AI app, then paste and save the result. RANKD does not connect to your AI provider or collect AI API keys. Shared reports are visible to workspace members; public links can be viewed by anyone who receives them.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and access information: an app-specific iCloud account identifier, sign-in/session credentials, workspace memberships and roles, and invitations. The app stores its session in the device Keychain.
  • Sleeper league information: league and user identifiers; league, team, and manager names; avatar URLs/images; rosters and eligible positions; scoring, matchups, standings, schedules, settings, and transaction information needed for the features you use. We obtain this through Sleeper's read-only API. RANKD does not receive your Sleeper password.
  • Saved app content: ranking formulas and presets, selected leagues, published ranking snapshots, saved AI reports and commentary settings, share links, publication preferences, and related activity/audit records.
  • Subscription and usage records: Apple transaction identifiers and signed receipts, product and subscription status, expiration/revocation information, an account token used to bind a subscription to your RANKD account, and AI-report allowance/usage records.
  • Technical and support information: IP addresses, request and security metadata, errors and operational logs handled by our hosting providers, and information you voluntarily send in a support request. Do not send passwords or payment card numbers.

2. Why we use this information

We use it to authenticate your account; import and refresh leagues; calculate rankings and analytics; save, sync, and share content; save writing you import; maintain necessary historical subscription records; provide support; prevent abuse; and maintain the reliability and security of the service. We do not provide advertisers with your iCloud account identifier or league content.

3. Sharing league content

Members of the same shared RANKD workspace can read its saved league reports, including AI reports generated by another member. Importing the same Sleeper league independently does not automatically join another person's RANKD workspace. Commissioners control official publishing and invitations. A public ranking link makes its included league/team information, rankings, avatars, and saved commentary available to anyone with the link; recipients can forward it.

Graphic exports are created on your device. When you share or save an image, the destination app or photo library receives that image. Images saved or forwarded outside RANKD cannot be recalled by deleting the original report or your RANKD account.

4. Write with My AI

RANKD prepares a prompt containing team IDs and calculated weekly statistics, including ranks, records, scores, average scoring, power ratings, and rank movements. Team and league names are optional; you can use numbered team labels instead. Manager names, avatars, iCloud account identifiers, passwords, and credentials are not included as separate fields. Review the prompt before copying it: a team or league name may itself identify a person.

Nothing is automatically sent to an AI provider. If you copy or share a prompt and paste it into another app, that app and its provider receive the content under their own terms, privacy policies, charges, and usage limits. RANKD does not collect API keys, use your AI account, or pay for these requests.

Pasted responses and prompt settings may be saved as private drafts on your device while you switch apps. Drafts are excluded from backup and cleared on sign-out. When you explicitly save a reviewed report, its writing and associated ranking snapshot are stored in your RANKD workspace and visible to its members. They can appear in public reports you choose to share. Review facts, tone, and personal information before saving or sharing. AI writing may be inaccurate.

Stat-based writing, local analytics, reading saved reports, and graphic exports do not generate AI requests. Reports generated by an earlier version remain readable; those earlier hosted requests were processed by OpenAI under its applicable policies, including its Privacy Policy. Historical provider security records may remain subject to their retention policies.

5. Free app and historical subscriptions

This iOS version offers no subscriptions or in-app purchases. All league tools are free; commissioner permissions still control official settings and publishing. If you previously subscribed, manage or cancel any existing subscription in Apple subscription settings. Updating or deleting RANKD does not itself cancel an existing Apple subscription.

We may retain historical Apple transaction identifiers, signed receipts, subscription status, account tokens, and allowance records for account support, security, and applicable obligations. We do not receive payment card numbers.

6. Advertising

This iOS version contains no advertising or Google AdMob/UMP SDKs and makes no ad requests. There is no advertising identifier or tracking permission request. Earlier builds may have contained advertising and consent SDKs; information previously handled by those providers remains subject to their policies, including Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Service providers and hosting

We use Apple iCloud/CloudKit for authentication and cloud records, Cloudflare for the API and public sharing pages, Apple services for historical purchase support. Earlier versions used OpenAI for hosted AI writing and included Google advertising/consent integrations. Your chosen external AI provider receives content only when you transfer it there yourself. The existing web app is also hosted on Vercel. This public policy/support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may receive ordinary web request information such as IP addresses and browser metadata. The support site has no RANKD analytics scripts, advertising, login forms, or marketing cookies.

Providers process data according to their terms and privacy policies: Apple, Cloudflare, Vercel, and GitHub. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may disclose necessary records to meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, protect users or the service, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate privacy protections.

8. Device storage and permissions

League data, saved workspace information, and personal formulas may be cached inside the app's protected device storage for offline viewing. Signing out clears the app's cached workspace file, local writing drafts, and saved session. Generated image files may remain temporarily in the app's private storage until the system removes them or the app is deleted. Images you save in Photos remain in your photo library.

The app requests permission to add images to Photos only when you choose Save to Photos; it does not require access to browse your photo library. You can use the share sheet without granting Photos permission. RANKD does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

9. Retention, deletion, and your choices

We retain account and workspace records while needed to provide your account and saved features, and retain necessary subscription, quota, security, and operational records for their service purpose or applicable obligations. There is no single fixed retention period for every provider or record type.

To delete your RANKD account, sign in and go to More → Delete account, then confirm. This removes your account's workspace index, owned workspaces, personal presets/membership in other workspaces, associated share links and sign-in grants, and applicable subscription entitlement records. Owned-workspace public links stop working. Some audit identifiers in workspaces owned by others are replaced with a deleted-account marker. League data imported or published independently by other users, copies others have exported, and records providers must retain are not removed by this action. Deletion does not change your Sleeper or Apple account.

For access, correction, deletion, privacy concerns, or other rights available under applicable law, contact hershapplications@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request without asking for your Apple password. If you cannot access the app, follow the account-deletion support instructions.

10. Security

RANKD uses HTTPS for network requests, Keychain storage for the native session, and encrypted cloud record payloads with service-side access checks. No internet service or device storage is completely secure. Keep your device and Apple account protected, and share workspace invitations and public links thoughtfully.

11. Children

RANKD is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to RANKD, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

12. Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or other appropriate notice where required. For privacy or support, email hershapplications@gmail.com.

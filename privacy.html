Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026 · Applies to RANKD for iOS and its related web services

RANKD is developed and operated by Ryan Hershberger ("RANKD," "we," or "us"). This policy explains the information used to provide fantasy football rankings, saved league workspaces, sharing, and optional RANKD Pro features. Privacy questions: ryankhershberger@gmail.com.

The essentials

Apple handles your iCloud sign-in and payments. We do not receive your Apple password or payment card details. Rankings and stat-based writing are calculated without an AI request. Optional AI reports send selected team labels and calculated statistics to OpenAI only after you confirm. Shared reports are visible to their workspace members, and public links can be viewed by anyone who receives them.

1. Information we handle

  • Account and access information: an app-specific iCloud account identifier, sign-in/session credentials, workspace memberships and roles, and invitations. The app stores its session in the device Keychain.
  • Sleeper league information: league and user identifiers; league, team, and manager names; avatar URLs/images; rosters and eligible positions; scoring, matchups, standings, schedules, settings, and transaction information needed for the features you use. We obtain this through Sleeper's read-only API. RANKD does not receive your Sleeper password.
  • Saved app content: ranking formulas and presets, selected leagues, published ranking snapshots, saved AI reports and commentary settings, share links, publication preferences, and related activity/audit records.
  • Subscription and usage records: Apple transaction identifiers and signed receipts, product and subscription status, expiration/revocation information, an account token used to bind a subscription to your RANKD account, and AI-report allowance/usage records.
  • Technical and support information: IP addresses, request and security metadata, errors and operational logs handled by our hosting providers, and information you voluntarily send in a support request. Do not send passwords or payment card numbers.
  • Advertising information, where ads are enabled: information processed by Google's advertising and consent SDKs, described below.

2. Why we use this information

We use it to authenticate your account; import and refresh leagues; calculate rankings and analytics; save, sync, and share content; verify subscriptions and report allowances; deliver optional AI reports; operate advertisements where enabled; provide support; prevent abuse; and maintain the reliability and security of the service. We do not provide advertisers with your iCloud account identifier or league content.

3. Sharing league content

Members of the same shared RANKD workspace can read its saved league reports, including AI reports generated by another member. Importing the same Sleeper league independently does not automatically join another person's RANKD workspace. Commissioners control official publishing and invitations. A public ranking link makes its included league/team information, rankings, avatars, and saved commentary available to anyone with the link; recipients can forward it.

Graphic exports are created on your device. When you share or save an image, the destination app or photo library receives that image. Images saved or forwarded outside RANKD cannot be recalled by deleting the original report or your RANKD account.

4. Optional AI reports

After you explicitly confirm generation, RANKD sends the selected week's team identifiers and team names, ranks, previous ranks, scores, win/loss/tie records, power ratings, and ranking movements to OpenAI to create league and team writeups. Manager names, avatar images, Apple account identifiers, passwords, and purchase details are not separate fields in the AI request. A team name may itself contain a person's name, so avoid including sensitive information in league labels.

Stat-based writing, local analytics, viewing saved AI text, and exporting saved writing do not generate AI requests. Generated writing is saved in the workspace for its members and may appear in a public publication you choose to share. RANKD disables optional response storage in its AI requests. Providers may still retain records in their security or abuse-monitoring systems. OpenAI processes requests under its applicable API data policies. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy. AI writing may be inaccurate; review it before sharing.

5. Subscriptions

Apple processes RANKD Pro payments. We verify Apple's signed transaction and subscription status to grant access, handle entitlement changes, and track report allowances. We do not receive your payment card number. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings or Apple's subscription settings. Deleting your RANKD account does not cancel a subscription.

6. Advertising and consent choices

The free iOS app includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where advertising is enabled, the app requests non-personalized ads. Non-personalized ads still involve data processing; they do not mean that no data is collected.

Google's SDKs may process IP address (including approximate location inferred from it), device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, app usage/performance information, and crash/diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate its services. RANKD does not request access to your device's advertising identifier through Apple's tracking permission prompt. We do not send iCloud account identifiers, league contents, or AI reports to AdMob. Pro removes ad placements.

Where applicable, consent messages appear before eligible ad requests. You can reopen available choices in More → Ad privacy choices. Ad availability and the choices offered depend on your location and Google's requirements. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures. If an ad/consent request fails, the app may show no ad.

7. Service providers and hosting

We use Apple iCloud/CloudKit for authentication and cloud records, Cloudflare for the API and public sharing pages, OpenAI for optional AI generation, and Google for advertising/consent when enabled. The existing web app is also hosted on Vercel. This public policy/support website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may receive ordinary web request information such as IP addresses and browser metadata. The support site has no RANKD analytics scripts, advertising, login forms, or marketing cookies.

Providers process data according to their terms and privacy policies: Apple, Cloudflare, Vercel, and GitHub. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. We may disclose necessary records to meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, protect users or the service, or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate privacy protections.

8. Device storage and permissions

League data, saved workspace information, and personal formulas may be cached inside the app's protected device storage for offline viewing. Signing out clears the app's cached workspace file and saved session. Generated image files may remain temporarily in the app's private storage until the system removes them or the app is deleted. Images you save in Photos remain in your photo library.

The app requests permission to add images to Photos only when you choose Save to Photos; it does not require access to browse your photo library. You can use the share sheet without granting Photos permission. RANKD does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise device location.

9. Retention, deletion, and your choices

We retain account and workspace records while needed to provide your account and saved features, and retain necessary subscription, quota, security, and operational records for their service purpose or applicable obligations. There is no single fixed retention period for every provider or record type.

To delete your RANKD account, sign in and go to More → Delete account, then confirm. This removes your account's workspace index, owned workspaces, personal presets/membership in other workspaces, associated share links and sign-in grants, and applicable subscription entitlement records. Owned-workspace public links stop working. Some audit identifiers in workspaces owned by others are replaced with a deleted-account marker. League data imported or published independently by other users, copies others have exported, and records providers must retain are not removed by this action. Deletion does not change your Sleeper or Apple account.

For access, correction, deletion, privacy concerns, or other rights available under applicable law, contact ryankhershberger@gmail.com. We may need to verify your request without asking for your Apple password. If you cannot access the app, follow the account-deletion support instructions.

10. Security

RANKD uses HTTPS for network requests, Keychain storage for the native session, and encrypted cloud record payloads with service-side access checks. No internet service or device storage is completely secure. Keep your device and Apple account protected, and share workspace invitations and public links thoughtfully.

11. Children

RANKD is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to RANKD, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

12. Policy updates and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Material changes will be communicated through the app or other appropriate notice where required. For privacy or support, email ryankhershberger@gmail.com.
